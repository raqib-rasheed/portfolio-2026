# Obsidian portfolio

Dark, tactile, one page. Career facts stay the public resume.

## Surface

- Background `#0A0A0C`. Bone type `#F4F1EA`. Accent `#C9EBD8`.
- Mesh of two radial gradients. One follows the pointer and settles. It does not loop.
- Glass tiles use a 1px border at `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` and a faint inset highlight.
- Syne for display. JetBrains Mono for specs, tags, and the dock.

## Interactions

- Headline shears with mouse velocity, then springs back. Touch scroll does not shear it.
- Hero card switches Deloitte, Medline, and Growfin. Each one is a sentence about the job.
- Cases expand in place. Each preview names the real work: the census, the email and its editor, and Edward Jones.
- The Deloitte case names the client, the modern investment-management project, the agentic workflow, and the SDLC agent.
- Dock links to work, the agent, roles, and mail. `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` opens the command list.

## Out

No phone number. No invented percentages. No custom cursor. No scroll hijacking. The Deloitte client is Edward Jones, named because Raqib asked for it.
